About Me

Skilled across both backend and frontend, and experienced in cross-platform mobile development with Flutter and Dart.

TokoRay

Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Platform

Not a single-seller store. TokoRay is a marketplace where many sellers trade under one platform, with real payment through Midtrans, shipping rates and courier tracking through RajaOngkir, multiple warehouses, platform commission, and payouts to seller bank accounts.

Behind it: a layered REST API with 36 domain modules and 60 data models, a 44-page operator console covering catalog, orders, refunds, disputes, vouchers, flash sales, payouts and feature flags, and a Flutter app for buyers. Every write on the money path is guarded by pessimistic locking and idempotency keys, and 1,223 automated tests keep one cart from ever becoming five orders.

RajaOngkir shipping

A ledger that cannot drift

Each store wallet is row-locked with SELECT … FOR UPDATE across commission, settlement, refund and payout.

Multi-tenant with store RBAC

Live chat and notifications
