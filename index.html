About Me

Fullstack Developer specializing in Laravel and modern web architecture, with hands-on experience delivering production-grade applications from database design through to hosting deployment. I work across backend and frontend with Laravel, Express.js, Node.js, React.js, and Next.js, building scalable and maintainable web applications, and I ship cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter and Dart.

Most of my work sits close to money and access control: multi-tier wallet ledgers, QR-based cashless payments, payment gateway and shipping integrations, role-based dashboards, and immutable audit trails. I build them on layered architectures with transaction-safe data handling, and I keep them covered by automated tests — 1,168 tests on the SCFS finance platform and 1,223 tests on the TokoRay marketplace.

Proven ability to connect web and mobile systems through RESTful APIs — Laravel and Express.js on the server, React.js, Next.js, and Flutter on the client — into single end-to-end products. Holds a Bachelor's degree in Informatics Engineering from Universitas Pasundan, combining strong engineering fundamentals with a drive to turn ideas into reliable, useful products.

TokoRay

Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Platform

Not a single-seller store. TokoRay is a marketplace where many sellers trade under one platform, with real payment through Midtrans, shipping rates and courier tracking through RajaOngkir, multiple warehouses, platform commission, and payouts to seller bank accounts.

Behind it: a layered REST API with 36 domain modules and 60 data models, a 44-page operator console covering catalog, orders, refunds, disputes, vouchers, flash sales, payouts and feature flags, and a Flutter app for buyers. Every write on the money path is guarded by pessimistic locking and idempotency keys, and 1,223 automated tests keep one cart from ever becoming five orders.
